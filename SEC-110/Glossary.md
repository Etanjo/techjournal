**WEEK 1**
- *The internet* is just the physical network of all the devices and computers connected to each other
-  *The web* is the collection of information that is accessed through the internet
- *Cyberspace* is (the combination of everything tech) a global domain within the information environment consisting of interdependent networks of information systems including the internet, telecommunications networks, computer systems, embedded processors and controllers.
- *Cybersecurity* is defined as the prevention of damage to, protection of, and restoration of computers, networks, electronic communication, etc. Also known as the ability to protect or defend from cyber attacks
**WEEK 2**
- *The CIA Triad* is the three primary aspects of protected information in cyber security (confidentiality, integrity, availability)
- *Confidentiality* refers to the property that information is only available/accessible to those who are authorized
- *Integrity* is the assurance that information is accurate, trustworthy, and only changed by the proper sources
- *Availability* is the idea that information remains accessible to the authorized parties
- *Software vulnerabilities* are flaws, bugs, glitches, or weaknesses found in software that can be exploited
- Cyber threats are an attempt to damage or disrupt a computer network or system, either physically or software, human or not.
- *A risk matrix* is a visual that categorizes threats by both how likely they are and how bad it would be if they were to happen 
- *Supply chain attacks* are attacks that are meant to slow down, stop, or increase costs of production.
- *Spear phishing* is a type of phishing attack targeted at specific individuals or organizations rather than general phishing attempts
