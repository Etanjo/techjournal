## Questions:
- You deploy pcs and switches by navigating to their proper section in the bottom left, then clicking on the device you want to deploy (shift click for multiple) then click where you want to place it.

- You connect these devices by navigating to the connections tab and select the type of wiring you need. End device to switch is done with copper straight-through cables. Switch to switch is done with copper crossover cables. You then clock on the two devices you want to connect. End device to switch uses the fast ethernet ports, switch to switch uses gigabit slots

- Once you've given the pcs their information using the ethernet port tab, you can open the command prompt by going to the pc and clicking on the command prompt window, which is in the desktop window.
