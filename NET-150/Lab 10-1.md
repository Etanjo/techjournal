## Goal:
The goal was to use packet tracer to understand multi-layer switches on simple networks

## Useful Instructions:
In order to setup the default gateways on a multi-layer switch, you need to add them on a port by port basis. Otherwise, inputting the ips and subnet masks is essentially the same. Each port needs a different ip, however. You can also enter simulation mode via the button on the bottom right, letting you step through processes and see PDU details
