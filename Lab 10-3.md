## Goal:
The goal of this lab was to further our understanding of routers and multiple networks through packet tracer

## Useful Instructions:
You can setup http packets by opening web on computers and typing in the ip of a server in the URL bar. On routers you can save the port information by going to the config/settings tab and click save on NVRAM. Afterwards, if the router power cycles, the information stays.
